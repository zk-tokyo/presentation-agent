# co-SNARK：複数者の秘密入力を 1 つの証明にまとめる

<div class="text-sm text-gray-500 mt-1 mb-4">通常の ZK は prover 1 名が witness 全体を知る前提。<strong>co-SNARK</strong> は witness を複数者で<strong>秘密分散したまま</strong>、共同で 1 つの公開検証可能な proof を作る（ZK + MPC）。</div>

<div class="flex items-stretch justify-center gap-2 max-w-6xl mx-auto text-center">
<div class="flex-1 p-3 bg-white rounded-xl border border-gray-200 shadow-sm"><div class="font-black text-gray-900 text-sm">各 party が witness の share</div><div class="text-xs text-gray-500 mt-1">誰も全体を知らない</div></div>
<div class="self-center text-amber-500 font-black">&rarr;</div>
<div class="flex-1 p-3 bg-amber-50 rounded-xl border border-amber-200"><div class="font-black text-gray-900 text-sm">MPC で prover を協調実行</div><div class="text-xs text-gray-600 mt-1">secret-sharing 上で証明生成</div></div>
<div class="self-center text-amber-500 font-black">&rarr;</div>
<div class="flex-1 p-3 bg-white rounded-xl border border-gray-200 shadow-sm"><div class="font-black text-gray-900 text-sm">1 つの SNARK proof</div><div class="text-xs text-gray-500 mt-1">succinct・公開検証可</div></div>
<div class="self-center text-amber-500 font-black">&rarr;</div>
<div class="flex-1 p-3 bg-white rounded-xl border border-gray-200 shadow-sm"><div class="font-black text-gray-900 text-sm">誰でも検証</div><div class="text-xs text-gray-500 mt-1">通常の ZK と同じ verify</div></div>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-3 gap-4 max-w-6xl mx-auto mt-5">
<div class="p-4 bg-white rounded-xl border border-gray-200 shadow-sm">
<div class="text-md font-black text-gray-900 mb-2">何が新しいか</div>
<div class="text-sm text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">「単独 prover が witness を全部知る」前提を外す。<strong>互いに入力を見せず</strong>に共同で証明できる。</div>
</div>
<div class="p-4 bg-white rounded-xl border border-gray-200 shadow-sm">
<div class="text-md font-black text-gray-900 mb-2">出力</div>
<div class="text-sm text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">普通の SNARK proof。verifier からは通常の ZK と区別がつかない（succinct・公開検証可）。</div>
</div>
<div class="p-4 bg-amber-50 rounded-xl border border-amber-200">
<div class="text-md font-black text-gray-900 mb-2">設計フロー上の位置</div>
<div class="text-sm text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">「正しさを公開検証 × <strong>秘匿入力を複数者が持ち寄る</strong>」枝 = co-SNARK（ZK + MPC）。</div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="absolute bottom-3 left-6 text-[10px] text-gray-400 leading-tight max-w-3xl">

</div>
